How it works

  1. 01

    The plasma

    The glowing ring is a plasma at fusion temperature. No material can touch it, so a magnetic field holds it in place. The field only confines it where the field lines run along its surface; where they cross the surface, the plasma leaks. Those places glow orange.

  2. 02

    The coils

    All of the field comes from the coils around the plasma. Drag the points on a coil to change its shape, and the field is recalculated from the coil currents (the Biot–Savart law). On most levels you shape a few coils and symmetry copies them around the machine.

  3. 03

    The limits

    Coils must keep a minimum distance from each other and from the plasma, and must not bend too sharply. A segment that breaks a limit turns red. Stars only count when no limit is broken.

  4. 04

    The research records

    Level 04 uses a published plasma shape (Landreman & Paul, 2022) with the setup of the stage-2 example in SIMSOPT, an open-source stellarator design code. The record levels use the exact setup of Wechsung et al. (PNAS 2022), whose coils are the best published for this plasma. ★★★ means within 2 % of their design; ✦ means better, confirmed by a finer check. Designs export in SIMSOPT's format.

The score is the field error ⟨|B·n|⟩ / ⟨|B|⟩: the average field crossing the plasma surface divided by the average field strength. Lower is better. From Level 2 on, Relax runs a numerical optimiser on your current design.
